### 🚽 WePee

I hate walking around the city and finding that every bathroom nearby requires you to pay for something first, has a ridiculously long line, is closed, or is just dirty and it sucks. I'm sure you do too.

WePee helps you discover bathrooms nearby, see what others think, and keep a personal ranking of the places you’ve tried. Share reviews with friends, find a better stop, and help put more bathrooms on the map.

Built for iPhone. Currently in beta.
